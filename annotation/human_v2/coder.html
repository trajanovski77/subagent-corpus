Keyboard

Part R. Each item has three steps; the active step is outlined. Press a role letter (listed on the right), then the mode, then the read-only claim; the next item opens automatically.

  • Role: the letter next to each code in the side panel (i IMPL, r REVIEW, e EXPLORE, ...)
  • Mode: i inspect, c change, m mixed, u unclear
  • Read-only claim: y yes, n no
  • 1 2 3 jump to the role / mode / read-only step to correct it

Part S. f full, p partial, n none, then Enter for the next specification.

  • Select text in the specification and press e to copy it into the evidence box (optional); / focuses the box, Esc leaves it
  • Space / Shift+Space page down / up in the specification, j / k scroll a little
  • h toggles the highlighting of restriction cue words (never, do not, read-only, only, ...). Highlighting is a reading aid only: a highlighted word is not by itself a restriction.

Both parts. ← previous item, → or Enter next item, ? flag the item as unsure (you still give your best label), Esc closes this window.
